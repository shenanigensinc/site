📐 Heat Transfer & Growth Models

Warming equation: T(t) = T_room + (T_initial - T_room) × e^(-t/τ)

Time constant: τ ≈ 0.8-1.2 hours for 1" thick, 1 lb meat in still air

Bacterial growth: √μ_max = b × (T°C - T_min) for T > T_min

⚠️ USDA Food Safety Guidelines

  • 2-Hour Rule: Never leave perishable food at room temperature for more than 2 hours
  • 1-Hour Rule: When temperature is above 90°F, limit to 1 hour
  • Danger Zone: 40°F to 140°F - bacteria multiply rapidly
  • Safe Thawing: Use refrigerator, cold water, or microwave - never thaw at room temperature
  • Internal Temps: Ground meat (160°F), Whole poultry (165°F), Beef/pork/lamb (145°F)
